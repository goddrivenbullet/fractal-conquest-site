This policy describes how the Fractal Conquest iPhone app (“the app”) handles information. It is written for the version of the game as of November 20, 2026.

We collect no personal data

The app does not collect personal information. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other identifier. There is no account, no sign-in, and no profile.

No analytics, advertising, or tracking

The app does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking software. It does not use tracking cookies, advertising identifiers, or similar technologies to follow you across apps or websites.

Game saves stay on your iPhone

When you play, the app stores the current campaign on the device (using the phone’s local storage, technically called AsyncStorage). That save includes the generated map, the state of the war, and optional admiral names you type on the title screen. This information is used only to continue your campaign on that same iPhone. It is not sent anywhere.

Starting a new campaign replaces the save on that phone. Deleting the app removes the save.

No network use

The game is self-contained. It does not contact a game server, and it does not send saves or any other information off the device. Pass-and-play happens on one phone, handed from one person to the next. There is no online play, chat, or leaderboard in this version.

Children

Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. This policy is about data practices only; it is not an age rating.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy or about the app, email Croutbenefits@gmail.com.

Changes

If the app’s practices change — for example, if a future version adds online play — this policy will be updated and the date at the top of the page will change.
