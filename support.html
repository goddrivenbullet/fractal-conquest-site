Fractal Conquest is a turn-based war game for iPhone. You and up to three other admirals — people passing one phone around, or the built-in fleet — fight over a generated archipelago. Armies take cities. Fleets cross the water. Fog hides everything you have not seen.

How to play

  • On the title screen, leave at least one admiral as Human (you can set others to AI), then tap Start campaign. If a campaign is already saved on the phone, tap Continue campaign.
  • Drag the map to look around. Pinch to zoom.
  • Tap your army (the colored piece on your city). Pale tiles are in reach. Gold tiles are cities you can capture. Tap a highlighted tile to move.
  • Next unit jumps to the next piece that still has movement. Wait leaves a unit where it is. Tap End turn when you are done.
  • Tap a city you own, or tap City orders while a unit is standing on it, to choose what it builds. Ships can only be built in a harbor (a city touching the sea). A Factory speeds that city. Industry speeds every city you own.
  • A Settler can found a new city on open land at least three tiles from every other city. Transports can carry settlers and armies to other islands.
  • Fighters show a fuel number. Land them on a friendly city or carrier before it hits zero, or they crash.
  • A battleship can shell an enemy army on land within two tiles. Submarines stay hidden until a ship is next to them, or a destroyer has them on sonar.
  • Pace (on the title screen, and during a match) sets movement, how fast pieces glide, and build speed. Those choices are saved with the campaign.
  • You win when every enemy city is yours.

Saves

The campaign saves itself on this iPhone. Closing the app and opening it again brings the war back. Starting a new campaign replaces the one saved on the phone. Saves are local only — they are not stored online, and they are not backed up to another device by the app. Deleting Fractal Conquest from the iPhone removes the save.

Pass-and-play

With two or more human admirals, the map is covered between turns so you cannot peek at each other’s fog. When you end your turn, the phone shows a “Pass the phone” screen. Hand the device to the next admiral, and continue only when that person is looking.

There is no online play, account, or chat in this version. Everyone who is human shares one iPhone.
